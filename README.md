## Why it lands

The setup leans on a universally recognised office pain: the printer that only jams when a deadline is near. The reader expects the usual complaint — broken hardware, bad luck, IT never fixing anything. Instead the turn reframes the jam as a virtue: the printer is not broken, it has *boundaries*. The joke quietly flips the office's real problem back onto its people — everyone else in the office says yes to everything, works late, and never pushes back, while the one machine that refuses to cooperate is the only one with a healthy limit. The punchline lands because it is both a surprise and, on reflection, uncomfortably true.
